when first testing the server with the SOMAXCONN and Thread pool the size of hardwareonly:
    1) most of the requests were declined on the OS level ( max backlog queue reach )
    2) I found that the threads spend the majority of their life time waiting instead of doing any actual work
        since i use blocking recv and Transfile


so I bumped up the max backlog queue to 65k connections without touching the threadpool and improved:
    1) No declined requests on OS level
    2) Requests were timed out for different reason ( spend too long in the backlog qeueu )
    3) Threads still spent most of its time waiting on blocking functions


Then in addition to the backlog change I increased the threadpool size to a few hundred threads ( reducing interleaving as much as possible )

    1) No delcined reqeust on OS level
    2) Majority of requests were served correctly
    3) still a dozen or so were timed out ( sometimes all of them would be served but it would depend on 
        jammed the cpu of the pc was so it was kinda random )
    4) max stays at around 19.9 second idk if thats related to a hardware limit or that heys timeout
